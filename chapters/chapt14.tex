\section{Realms of Earth}
Earth is the only planet in the solar system that has life. Life exists on Earth because of the presence of life-sustaining elements that are land, water and air.
\vspace{0.1in}
\par
All the things that surround us make up our environment. The environment has both living and non-living things in it. Living beings like plants and animals depend on the non-living environmental realms like soil, air and water to live. The realms or spheres of Earth are not isolated-they overlap each other. There are four realms of Earth.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Atmosphere (\textit{atmo for air})
    \item Hydrosphere (\textit{hydro for water})
    \item Lithosphere (\textit{litho for land})
    \item Biosphere (\textit{bio for living things})
\end{itemize}
\subsection{Atmosphere}
The word 'atmosphere' means the sphere of air. The atmosphere that envelopes Earth comprises a mixture of many gases like oxygen, carbon dioxide and nitrogen. The atmosphere also contains water vapour and dust particles.
\subsection{\textbf{Importance of the atmosphere}}
\begin{itemize}
    \item It provides air that all living things breathe. Humans and other animals use oxygen present in the atmosphere for their survival.
    \item It keeps Earth warm by not lettering the heat escape at night.
    \item The atmosphere prevents the harmful rays of the Sun from reaching the surface of Earth.
    \item It prevent extreme temperature differences between day and night.
    \item Air is the medium that carries sound waves and helps us talk and hear. It also helps things like birds and kites to fly.
    \item The atmosphere maintains a balance that is important for maintaining life on Earth.
\end{itemize}
\subsection{Lithosphere}
The \textbf{lithosphere} is the solid outer part of Earth. It is the thinnest layer of Earth. The land we live on is part of this solid sphere.
\subsection{\textbf{Importance to the lithosphere}}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Lithosphere provides the land for us to live on. Forests, grasslands, land for agriculture and human settlements stand on this layer.
    \item It provides soil for farming. It is home to many living beings.
    \item It is a rich source of minerals like iron, copper, aluminium, coal and petroleum. These are important materials that we use for various things in our daily lives.
\end{itemize}
\subsection{Hydrosphere}
The \textbf{hydrosphere} is the mass of water found on, under and above the surface of Earth.
\vspace{0.1in}
\par
Earth is known as the blue planet as about 71 percent of it is covered with water and 29 percent is land. Water is found in soil, glaciers, ice caps, seas, oceans, rivers, lakes, ponds and streams. Water evaporates from water bodies, the vapours condense in the sky and water falls back on Earth as rain, hail and snow.
\subsection{\textbf{Importance of the hydrosphere}}
Water sustains various life forms. Therefore, hydrosphere is important for us in many ways.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Hydrosphere provides habitat for many plants and animals to live.
    \item It fulfils the basic need of living beings-water. It provides water to human beings, for drinking, domestic use and industrial use.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Biosphere}
\textit{Bios} is a Greek word which means life. Life cannot exist without land, water and air. \textbf{Biosphere} is the unique zone of life on Earth where the other three realms of Earth-the lithosphere, the hydrosphere and the atmosphere-interact with one another and support all life forms.
\vspace{0.1in}
\par
It provides all necessary things for the survival of life on Earth.
\subsection{\textbf{Importance of the biosphere}}
\begin{itemize}
    \item It is an area where life exists on, above and below Earth's surface.
    \item It is the natural habitat of all living organisms ranging from microorganisms to human beings, huge animals and plants.
    \item It provides food and raw material to plants, animals and humans.
\end{itemize}
\section{Interaction of Realms of Earth}
Scientists describe Earth in terms of spheres or systems. The realms or spheres of Earth are not isolated-they overlap each other. The biosphere gets sunlight and air from atmosphere. It collects water from oceans, rivers and other water bodies, called the hydrosphere. Also, biosphere is a region where life exists on the surface of Earth, which we call the lithosphere.
\vspace{0.1in}
\par
All living beings, including humans, are linked to the biosphere for their survival. Various human activities have altered the biosphere.
\vspace{0.6in}
\par
Humans have harmed the biosphere in many ways including the following.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Deforestation has led to the loss of habitats of many animals, birds, insects, etc. and caused the extinction of many speices.
    \item The air being polluted by emission of harmful gases from various factories and vehicles.
    \item Water is being polluted by dumping of domestic and industrial waste into water bodies. This has destroyed marine life.
\end{itemize}
\par
Such carries activities of humans have disturbed the balance in nature.

\end{itemize}
